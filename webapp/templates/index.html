Cross-domain recommendations from real Amazon ratings

Uses everything a real user liked in the source domain. Their own ratings are hidden from the model, so you can compare the picks with what they actually liked.

A brand-new user: search the source catalogue, pick a few titles you like, then get recommendations.

    Leave-one-user-out test: for each sampled user, recommend from their source likes (with their own data removed) and check against what they really liked in the target domain. Compared with a popularity baseline.

    Everything in this page uses these JSON endpoints.

    GET/api/healthStatus and loaded domains
    GET/api/domainsItems and users per domain
    GET/api/userssource, target, limit, offset, random
    GET/api/users/<id>Profile of one user
    GET/api/items/searchdomain, q, limit
    POST/api/recommend{source_domain, target_domain, k, user_id | liked_asins}
    GET/api/evaluatesource, target, k, n
    curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/recommend \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"source_domain":"Books","target_domain":"Movies_and_TV","k":5}'
    Data-based engine: “users who liked this also liked” co-occurrence across domains. No LLM, no Ollama.
